Community Guidelines

Effective: 2026-09-01 · Sognia

Sognia's public space is small on purpose: safe, anonymous, kind.

  • Share only what you are comfortable making public. Shared cards are sanitized, but the feeling is yours.
  • No harassment, hate, threats, or targeting of any person or group.
  • No sexual content involving minors — zero tolerance, reported where required.
  • No personal data of yourself or others (full names, addresses, phone numbers) in shared content.
  • No spam, scams, or commercial promotion.
  • Use Report to flag content and Block to stop seeing someone. Reports are reviewed; violating content is hidden or removed.

Repeated or severe violations can suspend or ban an account.
