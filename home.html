Dream

The part you were about to forget

Write it down while it is still there. Sognia reads the symbols and the feeling underneath them, and gives the dream back as something you can sit with — with an image that holds the scene, so the dream has a face when you come back to it months later.

Manifest

Say what you want. Then stand in it.

An intention written as a wish stays a wish. Sognia turns it into one specific moment after it has already happened — close enough to picture, with the light and the sound and the weight of it — a line to carry through the day, and one small step for tomorrow that you could genuinely take.

Map

What keeps coming back

Entries accumulate. Over weeks the themes that repeat rise to the surface on a map drawn from your own records — the drawing itself uses no AI. Nothing is diagnosed and nothing is predicted; you are simply shown what you have already been writing about.

Archive

Somewhere to put a year

Every reading stays. Search it, filter it by month, open a night from last spring and find it exactly as you left it. Private unless you decide otherwise.

Shared, carefully

Only what you choose to show

If a reading is worth sharing, Sognia publishes a sanitized version of it under an anonymous name — never your original words, never your identity. Everything else stays where you wrote it.

Privacy

Yours, unless you decide otherwise

Read the Privacy Policy
  • Private by designWhat you write stays yours. An entry becomes public only if you choose to publish a sanitized version of it.
  • Publicly anonymousInside Sognia you appear as an auto-generated name. Your Google or Apple identity is never shown and never attached to it.
  • Nothing to fend offThere are no public comments and no direct messages.
  • AI-assisted, and we say soReadings and images are generated. They are reflective — not predictive, and not advice.
